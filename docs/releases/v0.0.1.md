# PSGopher v0.0.1

The first release of PSGopher: Gopher64, the Nintendo 64 emulator, as a native PlayStation 5 title
(`PPSA99280`) for jailbroken consoles running homebrew. A very early build: expect bugs.

## Added
- **Gopher64's emulation on the PS5**: its VR4300 and RSP interpreters and parallel-rdp, the Vulkan
  compute RDP, on RADV linked into the title. Resolution 1x to 8x, SSAA downscaling, the CRT shader,
  widescreen, integer scaling, overclock, rewind and every other Gopher64 setting.
- **The Live Board**: the home screen is a live N64 mainboard with your cartridge seated in its slot.
  HOME (the recently played strip), RACK (every cartridge, A to Z, L2/R2 by letter, sorts and
  filters) and BENCH (the settings, the board's parts).
- **Games from anywhere**: the console's storage, USB drives and SMB/NFS network shares. A game on a
  share is copied to the console first, through a helper that keeps the copy fast.
- **Per-game settings** on a game's page (Triangle): every video, sound, emulation and controller
  setting, Use Global Setting by default.
- **Probes** (save states) with a picture of their moment, and the quick menu in a game (tap the
  touch pad): resume, take or load a probe, the stats overlay, reset, eject.
- **Picture options**: Sharp Bilinear scaling (the default), the VI filters, deinterlacing, overscan
  crop, downsampling and HDR10.
- **The Expansion Pak by game**: Auto turns it off only for the cartridges listed as needing 4 MB,
  and each game's need is shown on the board.
- **Cheats**, configurable **hotkeys**, cartridge **covers** and **descriptions**, and **updates**
  from inside the title (BENCH > Console > Check for Updates).

## Tested
On a PS5: test builds of this code were played. `NREE`, `NCFE`, `NN6E` and `NSME` ran at 100% at 8x,
and so did a PAL game; `NCFE`'s menu, which ran at 68% before this release's interpreter work, runs
at 100% and 60 fps at 8x. Also played: network shares (SMB), a USB drive, the sandbox exit, copies
through the helper, covers, descriptions, the quick menu, the board's navigation, per-game settings
and the one-time warning. On a PC: the host tests, the interface's pictures, the check of the
settings' words, and the lockstep harness, which runs the interpreters against Gopher64's own.

## Installation
- Copy the `PPSA99280` folder to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, over an older one if you have it.
- Your games, saves, probes and settings are kept (they are in `/data/psgopher`).
- Read `README.md` and `NOTICE.txt` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
- Open an issue and attach `psgopher-boot.log` from the title folder. Don't attach games, BIOS
  files, firmware or keys.
- If a game's picture is wrong, try it with its picture settings back at their defaults on its
  page (Triangle), and say whether that changed it.
