# PSGopher v0.0.1

The first release of PSGopher: Gopher64, the Nintendo 64 emulator, as a native PlayStation 5 title
(`PPSA99280`) for jailbroken consoles running homebrew. **A very early build**: expect bugs.

Download `PSGopher-v0.0.1.zip`, check it against `SHA256SUMS`, and copy its `PPSA99280` folder to
`/data/homebrew/` (or wherever your loader runs titles from).

## Added
- **Gopher64's emulation on the PS5**: its VR4300 and RSP interpreters and parallel-rdp (the
  Vulkan compute RDP) on RADV, linked into the title. Resolution 1x to 8x, SSAA downscaling, the CRT
  shader, widescreen, integer scaling, overclock, rewind and every other Gopher64 setting.
- **The Live Board**: the home screen is a live N64 mainboard with your cartridge seated in its
  slot. HOME (the recently played strip), RACK (every cartridge, A to Z, L2/R2 by letter, sorts and
  filters) and BENCH (the settings, the board's parts).
- **Your games from anywhere**: the console's storage, USB drives and SMB/NFS network shares. A game
  on a share is copied to the console first, through a helper that keeps the copy fast.
- **Per-game settings** on a game's page (Triangle): every video, sound, emulation and controller
  setting, "Use Global Setting" by default.
- **Probes** (save states) with a picture of their moment, and the quick menu in a game (tap the
  touch pad): resume, take or load a probe, the stats overlay, reset, eject.
- **Picture options**: Sharp Bilinear scaling (the default), VI filters, deinterlacing, overscan
  crop, downsampling, and HDR10.
- **The Expansion Pak by game**: Auto turns it off only for the cartridges listed as needing 4 MB;
  each game's need is shown on the board.
- **Cheats**, configurable **hotkeys**, cartridge **covers** and **descriptions**, and **updates**
  from inside the title (BENCH > Console > Check for Updates).

## Tested on a console
- Five NTSC and PAL games (`NREE`, `NCFE`, `NN6E`, `NSME` and a PAL game) at full speed at 8x,
  including a demanding menu that ran at 68% before this release's interpreter work.
- Network shares (SMB), USB drives, the sandbox exit, copies through the write helper, covers,
  descriptions, the quick menu, the board's navigation and the one-time warning.
